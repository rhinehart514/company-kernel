# Think Further

Ask what the company is still thinking too small about.

Use `$venture-judgment`. Load worldview, strategy, product, GTM, research,
operating, and any other reference that can change the call.

Read `PROJECT.md`, `NOW.md`, the product, repository, market evidence, and
recent learning before generating directions.

## Find the local maximum

Identify where the current plan may be constrained by:

- yesterday's cost of software or labor
- inherited category boundaries
- a wedge mistaken for the final company
- a roadmap made of incremental requests
- a product and GTM split that should collapse
- unnecessary human coordination
- a service boundary the product can now absorb
- fear of running several cheap, attributable bets
- a capability the product has not turned into responsibility
- distribution treated as a campaign instead of a product asset

Do not assume bigger means more features.

The stronger move may be a larger outcome with a smaller interface.

## Generate coherent trajectories

Return three to five materially different trajectories.

Each trajectory must state:

1. **The possibility:** what becomes possible for the customer or company
2. **Why now:** which capability, cost change, market shift, or earned asset enables it
3. **Connection:** why it is the same company rather than adjacent distraction
4. **Compounding path:** what gets stronger with each use, customer, or deployment
5. **Required truth:** the assumptions that must hold
6. **Kill condition:** evidence that should end the bet
7. **First proof:** the fastest credible move toward reality
8. **Product and GTM motion:** how the product and market test reinforce each other

Do not produce an idea landfill.

Do not vary only the feature set.

Do not present options without a call.

## Recommend the strongest direction

Choose the trajectory with the best combination of:

- size of outcome
- timing
- access
- learning speed
- proof density
- product coherence
- responsible ownership
- compounding advantage
- reversibility of the first move

Explain why it wins and what the company should stop doing to make room.

Ambition requires concentration after the possibility is found.

## Move from idea to evidence

For the recommended trajectory, define:

- the smallest credible test
- what can run in parallel
- what must be sequenced
- the actor to reach
- the artifact or behavior to ship
- the commitment to seek
- the proof threshold
- the review date or event

Do not confuse a smaller prototype with a faster learning loop.

## Context discipline

Ideas are cheap. Permanent context is expensive.

Do not update `PROJECT.md` or `NOW.md` because a possibility was generated.

Update them only after the user accepts a direction or evidence changes the
current model.

Rejected ideas disappear.

A sophisticated archive of abandoned possibilities is still clutter.
