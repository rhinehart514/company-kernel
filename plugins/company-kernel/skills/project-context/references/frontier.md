# Frontier

Intelligence and software production are becoming abundant.

Judgment, taste, attention, trust, distribution, proprietary context, physical execution, and contact with reality are not.

Many product boundaries, workflows, company structures, and pieces of startup advice were shaped by expensive intelligence and expensive software. They are historical, not natural.

Markets reorganize more slowly than capabilities change. The gap matters.

Cheap execution should increase ambition, not merely increase output.

A tiny team can attempt a company that recently required an organization. Headcount is not a proxy for seriousness.

The current product can be narrow while the destination company is enormous.

The wedge is where reality permits entry. It is not a ceiling.

The strongest ambitious companies are structurally legible. Winning the current loop gives them data, authority, trust, distribution, capability, or system position that makes the next loop easier to own.

The largest company may have the smallest product sentence.

A user should understand what this is, what it does, what happens next, and why to trust it without learning the company's internal machinery.

Capability should often expand while visible complexity contracts.

When implementation is cheap, coherence becomes a primary constraint.

A useful feature can still be the wrong feature. The product should become more obviously itself.

Some tools should become operators. Some software companies should absorb service. Some services should acquire software economics. Some workflows should disappear.

Outcome ownership is powerful only where authority, reliability, verification, trust, and economics support it.

Generated communication makes generic persuasion cheaper. Proof, specificity, timing, access, and product experience become more valuable.

GTM can be a product and an engineering surface. The ambition is not more messages. It is stronger market sensing, proof, reach, learning, and compounding distribution.

Reality outranks inherited context.

Behavior is stronger than stated preference. Payment is stronger than interest. Repeated use is stronger than launch excitement. Retention is stronger than usage without consequence.

An attractive belief should be easy to kill with strong evidence.

Context accumulates truth debt when old assumptions, expired technical limits, and model-generated claims survive after their rationale disappears.

The company boundary remains provisional.

Build for the world that exists now.
