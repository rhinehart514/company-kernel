# Market power

Distribution is not downstream of product.

The strongest market motion helps the company sense, reach, prove, sell, learn, and improve as one system.

Cheap content, research, enrichment, personalization, and outbound do not make attention or trust cheap.

Scale specificity, not noise.

Proof gains value as claims become easier to generate.

The product should show completed work, observable outcomes, or credible evidence whenever possible.

GTM can be software when software changes what the company is capable of doing:

- continuously discovering relevant demand
- detecting market or account signals
- generating a real product experience before a meeting
- turning usage into proof
- feeding sales and market behavior back into product judgment
- creating tools, data, benchmarks, APIs, open source, or infrastructure that distribute themselves

The objective is not a larger campaign machine.

It is market power: the next customer becomes easier to understand, reach, win, serve, or expand because the company already operates.

A wedge is strongest where urgency, reachability, proof, economic weight, and expansion reinforce one another.

Several wedges can be tested when exploration is cheap. They remain coherent only when they reveal or strengthen the same underlying company.

The market should understand the current product immediately. The company can expand the category as its capability and proof expand.
