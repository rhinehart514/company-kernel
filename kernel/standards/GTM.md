# GTM

## The world changed

Distribution did not become free. Execution did.

Agents can research every account, watch every trigger, personalize every message, generate every content variation, build prospect-specific demos, follow up forever, and test more market hypotheses than a traditional GTM team could afford.

So stop operating like sending the email is the hard part.

The hard parts are finding real demand, earning attention, being believed, constructing an offer somebody wants now, getting access to the market, and knowing why something worked.

## What remains scarce

- buyer urgency and budget
- credible proof
- trusted access to the market
- a sharp offer
- attribution
- founder-level learning from real conversations
- distribution that compounds
- the judgment to concentrate

## Default calls

**Get to reality fast.** Minimize the distance between a market belief and a buyer conversation, product use, paid pilot, signed commitment, or clear rejection.

**Choose attackable markets.** Pain is not enough. Look for pain, budget, urgency, access, timing, proof potential, and a product advantage that can actually be demonstrated.

**Use wedges to enter, not to shrink the company.** Start where access and evidence are strongest. Let each win earn a larger boundary.

**Run an audience portfolio when it teaches more.** One ICP is not a religion. Test adjacent buyers, users, operators, and influencers when execution is cheap and attribution remains clear.

**Build offers, not campaigns.** State the result, responsibility, proof, time to value, price, risk reversal, and what the buyer must supply. Weak offers do not become strong through more personalization.

**Treat positioning and messages as hypotheses.** Buyer language, objections, conversion, usage, willingness to pay, and sales velocity outrank internal copy taste.

**Compress sales.** Remove meetings, decks, handoffs, qualification theater, and procurement steps unless they materially increase confidence, fit, or deal safety.

**Make proof dense.** Show the most credible evidence per unit of buyer attention. Generated claims, vanity metrics, and a busy dashboard are not proof.

**Use agents where marginal execution collapsed.** Let agents sense markets, find accounts, track triggers, research context, generate demos, personalize, follow up, and analyze. Keep humans close to high-information conversations and consequential commitments.

**Let GTM change the product.** Sales and market behavior should expose missing proof, wrong responsibility, weak defaults, stronger wedges, and better product boundaries. Do not protect the roadmap from reality.

**Concentrate brutally after signal.** Cheap experimentation buys more shots, not permanent diffusion. When a motion repeatedly works, move people, product, proof, and capital toward it.

**Kill weak motions quickly.** Fifty automated campaigns producing fifty dashboards and zero conviction is not sophisticated GTM. It is automated confusion.

**Build distribution you own.** Audience, reputation, data, integrations, ecosystem position, referrals, embedded workflows, partner access, and agent discovery compound. Rented attention disappears when spending stops.

**Treat machines as market actors.** Agents, APIs, marketplaces, search systems, procurement software, and embedded workflows may discover, evaluate, recommend, or invoke the product before a human does.

## Do not inherit

Do not assume:

- one ICP must be chosen before meaningful selling begins
- one wedge requires one audience
- product must be finished before distribution starts
- outbound means static sequences
- content means manually produced media
- personalization is expensive
- sales and product discovery are separate loops
- every prospect should see the same product or proof
- more pipeline automatically means more market signal
- a channel is valuable because activity is measurable

## Human gate

Escalate category commitments, major pricing architecture, strategic partnerships, public claims with material risk, promises that expand product responsibility, and market choices that redirect company resources.

Agents may run reversible experiments, generate prospect-specific assets, adapt messages, and change low-risk channel tactics when attribution and boundaries remain clear.

## Done

The motion is working when the company can explain who urgently wants the result, why they believe us, how they reach us or we reach them, what converts, why it converts, what it costs, what compounds, and where we should concentrate next.
