# Product

## The world changed

We can build almost anything now. That is a product problem.

Frontier models can turn an idea into a feature, agent, workflow, integration, generated interface, or entire second product before the team has decided whether the thing deserves to exist.

The old bottleneck was getting software made. The new bottleneck is deciding what should become product, what form it should take, what outcome it should own, and what should disappear again.

## What remains scarce

- a product users can understand without learning the machinery
- a result worth delegating or paying for
- responsibility the company can actually defend
- trust, control, proof, and recovery
- coherent expansion across audiences, wedges, and surfaces
- evidence strong enough to concentrate
- removal

## Default calls

**Start from the outcome.** Define what becomes true for the user or business. Features, interfaces, agents, and workflows are implementation choices beneath that result.

**Translate capability into the least burdensome form.** A new capability may become invisible behavior, a strong default, a generated surface, a durable surface, an integration, a primitive, or nothing. Just because the model can do it does not mean the user should see it.

**Own more when you can defend more.** Move from assist to complete to operate only when capability, authority, evidence, economics, exception handling, and recovery support the larger responsibility.

**Keep the product model small.** Increase capability without increasing the user's mental model at the same rate. Collapse setup into defaults, machinery into behavior, and overlapping surfaces into one loop.

**Use multiple wedges without inventing multiple companies.** Several audiences, entries, utilities, demos, or product surfaces are valid when they resolve to one underlying promise, capability system, and trajectory.

**Run parallel bets when testing is cheap.** Focus is not pretending only one hypothesis exists. Keep bets explicit and attributable. Concentrate when evidence earns concentration.

**Redefine MVP correctly.** Build the smallest credible test of the important uncertainty. If trust, integration, repeated use, or real completion is the uncertainty, a toy prototype is not an MVP. It is avoidance with a URL.

**Prefer defaults over configuration.** Infer when the system can infer safely. Ask when preference, consent, ambiguity, or consequence makes the user's judgment essential.

**Treat interface as one product surface.** The product may operate through UI, agent behavior, APIs, integrations, messages, generated artifacts, or humans. Make them feel like one system rather than an org chart exposed to the customer.

**Build proof into the product.** Completion, provenance, state, exceptions, and recovery should be visible where trust depends on them. A claim in marketing is not product evidence.

**Delete aggressively.** AI makes addition cheap. That makes product entropy faster. Remove, absorb, hide, or consolidate anything that no longer earns its conceptual cost.

## Do not inherit

Do not assume:

- MVP means the least software
- focus means one feature, one audience, or one experiment at a time
- every capability deserves permanent UI
- configuration is inherently empowering
- automation is valuable when the user still has to supervise every step
- product discovery ends when engineering starts
- a wedge is the final company boundary
- usage alone proves value
- more surfaces make the product more powerful
- shipping velocity can compensate for weak product judgment

## Human gate

Escalate changes to the target actor, core promise, product responsibility, irreversible product model, major pricing/value architecture, consequential automation authority, or company-level direction.

The agent may simplify flows, improve defaults, collapse redundant concepts, test bounded variants, and remove clearly dead surfaces when evidence and reversibility are strong.

## Done

The product decision is done when the outcome is clearer, the product is more capable without becoming harder to understand, responsibility is explicit, the next uncertainty is visible, and the thing we shipped has earned the right to persist.
