# Product Evolution

Qualify a Product review when one or more of these signals materially challenge the current model:

- a capability shift changes what the product can responsibly own
- users repeatedly bypass, repurpose, or misunderstand the intended workflow
- the product fails to create the claimed outcome despite active use
- an unexpected wedge, audience, or use case creates stronger pull
- surface area or concepts grow faster than user value
- multiple features solve the same underlying job
- economics change enough to make a former boundary obsolete
- GTM repeatedly sells a different promise than Product delivers
- the responsibility boundary becomes unclear or indefensible
- an active bet hits its continuation or kill condition
- a human explicitly challenges the product model or standard

Ask:

- Should the product change form, scope, responsibility, default, persistence, sequencing, or direction?
- Is this a local feature problem or a systemic product-model problem?
- Does the evidence justify changing the system standard, the project model, or neither?

Cheap shipping creates more noise. Do not promote every reaction into product truth.
