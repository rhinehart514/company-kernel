# GTM Evolution

Qualify a GTM review when one or more of these signals materially change the market attack:

- buyer behavior, urgency, budget, or buying process shifts
- a new distribution surface creates real access or compounding leverage
- channel economics materially improve or collapse
- AI capability enables a new offer, proof format, sales motion, or machine-mediated channel
- unexpected inbound or conversion reveals stronger demand elsewhere
- one wedge or audience materially outperforms the current concentration
- sales cycles compress, expand, or fail for a repeated reason
- willingness to pay or packaging response changes
- proof becomes easier, harder, or newly required
- competition, platforms, procurement, or regulation changes the valid motion
- product usage reveals a different buyer, outcome, or category
- a human explicitly challenges the current market thesis or standard

Ask:

- Should market position, offer, audience, message, channel, proof, sales motion, concentration, or Product change?
- Is the signal attributable, repeated, and economically meaningful?
- Are we seeing demand, attention, or merely automated activity?

A spike in replies is not a company strategy.
