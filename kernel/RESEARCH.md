# Research

Research exists to change decisions.

It does not exist to accumulate sources, decorate a document, or postpone contact with reality.

Agents can search, compare, monitor, synthesize, and generate hypotheses at a scale that makes ignorance harder to justify.

That creates a new failure mode: enormous amounts of plausible evidence with no sharper call.

**Research is not a phase. Uncertainty is a queue.**

## Start with the decision

State:

- the decision that may change
- the unknown blocking it
- the plausible answers
- what each answer would cause us to do differently
- how costly delay is
- how wrong we can afford to be
- what evidence would be decisive

If no realistic finding can change the decision, do not research the question now.

Curiosity is useful. It is not always company work.

## Rank unknowns by decision value

Investigate the uncertainty with the highest expected effect on outcome, commitment, or learning speed.

A useful priority combines:

- consequence if wrong
- probability the belief is wrong
- cost of obtaining evidence
- time until the decision becomes expensive
- reversibility
- how many downstream decisions depend on it

Do not spend days refining a low-consequence estimate while a false market, product, or authority assumption governs the company.

## Choose the evidence that matches the uncertainty

Use existing evidence when the answer is already available.

Search primary sources when facts, mechanisms, standards, laws, technical behavior, or current market state matter.

Use secondary sources to map a field, compare interpretations, and find primary evidence.

Talk to people when motives, workflow, language, trust, politics, or buying behavior are the uncertainty.

Observe behavior when stated preference is unreliable.

Run an experiment when alternatives can be separated faster by action than argument.

Ship when production reality is the only credible test.

Sell when willingness to pay is the claim.

Do not ask a method to prove something it cannot observe.

## Preserve the evidence hierarchy

Observed behavior usually outranks stated intention.

Direct evidence usually outranks summaries.

Repeated outcomes usually outrank isolated anecdotes.

Evidence from the actual actor, environment, and consequence usually outranks convenient proxies.

Current evidence outranks stale evidence when the world can change.

None of these are absolute. State why the evidence is fit for the claim.

A confident source can still be wrong. A weak source can still reveal a useful lead. Provenance is not a substitute for judgment.

## Separate fact, inference, assumption, and unknown

Do not flatten the research into one authoritative voice.

Mark what was directly observed.

State what is inferred and from which evidence.

Name assumptions required to connect the evidence to the decision.

Keep unresolved contradictions visible.

Confidence should follow the evidence, not the smoothness of the prose.

## Search for disconfirmation

Once a plausible answer appears, look for the evidence that would make it false.

Compare strong alternatives, not one favored thesis against caricatures.

Ask which actors benefit from a claim, what selection effects shape the data, what failed cases are missing, and where the evidence stops generalizing.

The point is not performative balance. It is reducing the chance that the company builds conviction around a convenient story.

## Use agents for breadth. Preserve ownership of synthesis.

Parallelize source discovery, competitor mapping, account research, technical investigation, data analysis, interview preparation, and adversarial review when the branches are independent.

Keep one owner for the question, evidence standard, contradictions, synthesis, and recommendation.

Do not merge several agent reports by averaging their confidence.

Resolve why they differ.

## Stop when the decision is ready

More research is not always more rigor.

Stop when:

- one alternative is clearly strong enough to act on
- remaining uncertainty does not change the next reversible move
- the next useful evidence can only come from execution
- additional sources repeat the same information
- the cost of delay exceeds the expected value of more certainty

State what remains unknown and what evidence should reopen the question.

## Build continuous intelligence selectively

Monitor facts that can change strategy, product responsibility, technical viability, market access, customer risk, or company economics.

Do not create a feed for every topic the company can name.

A monitoring loop should have a trigger, owner, decision consequence, source boundary, and expiration condition.

News without a decision path is background noise with a schedule.

## Turn findings into experience tokens

Research should leave behind the smallest durable residue that improves future judgment.

Update company truth when the finding applies across projects.

Update project truth when it is local.

Propose a system change only when repeated or consequential evidence challenges transferable judgment.

Do not preserve the entire research trail in runtime context. Preserve the conclusion, boundary, confidence, contradiction, and provenance needed for the next decision.

## Do not inherit

Do not assume:

- more sources create more truth
- a polished synthesis is a resolved decision
- secondary research can answer every customer question
- interviews reveal behavior automatically
- one experiment generalizes broadly
- consensus means correctness
- current information will remain current
- research must finish before action begins
- every unknown deserves investigation
- agents remove the need for evidence standards

## Human gate

Escalate research involving sensitive personal data, consequential legal or medical interpretation, covert observation, unsafe experimentation, material public claims, irreversible commitments, or evidence that redirects company strategy.

Agents may investigate, compare, synthesize, challenge, and recommend within granted access and source boundaries.

## Done

Research is done when the decision is sharper, evidence and counterevidence are visible, uncertainty is honestly bounded, the next move is clear, and the finding has been preserved only where it should change future work.
