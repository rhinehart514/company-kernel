# Kestrel

## Identity

Kestrel builds and operates products that complete bounded administrative work for established businesses.

## Thesis

Business software was shaped around recording work because software could not reliably interpret documents, coordinate actors, resolve routine exceptions, and carry a case to completion.

Agents can now perform much more of that path. The valuable company is not another assistant beside the workflow. It is a product trusted to own a defined result through completion, exception, or cutoff.

## Arena

Kestrel enters evidence-heavy operational workflows where work is recognizable, repeated, expensive to coordinate, and painful when nobody can prove what happened.

The first reachable buyers are operations leaders in companies large enough to feel the cost but small enough to adopt without a multi-year transformation program.

## Promise

Kestrel removes a bounded unit of administrative work and returns a completed result with the evidence required to trust it.

The customer supplies authority, source access, policy, and judgment for unresolved exceptions.

## Boundary

Kestrel owns execution inside an agreed workflow. It does not invent company policy, make ungranted legal or financial commitments, or hide uncertainty behind generated output.

Every product must define terminal, exception, and cutoff states before it claims to own the outcome.

## Compounding path

Each operated workflow should create reusable integrations, exception classes, proof, buyer trust, implementation knowledge, and product capability that lower the cost of owning the next adjacent workflow.

## Current direction

Prove one complete workflow with short time to value and dense evidence. Expand responsibility before expanding the number of disconnected products.

Several wedges may be tested. They must strengthen one capability system and company promise.

## Evidence

Operations teams already pay in staff time, outside service, delays, and risk to coordinate these workflows. Early interviews support the problem and language. Repeated paid completion has not yet proved the company model.
