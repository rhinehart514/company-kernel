# Project

> Operate one expensive business workflow end to end while people retain consequential judgment and proof of every result.

## Outcome

A customer delegates repeated work and receives a completed, verifiable result instead of another tool to supervise.

## Why now

Frontier agents can perform longer-horizon work across documents and systems. Most products still expose the machinery or stop before the outcome.

## Boundary

The product owns agreed execution and proof inside granted authority. The customer owns source access, policy, ambiguous exceptions, and irreversible commitments outside that authority.

## Core loop

Delegate work, execute, surface exceptions, complete, prove, and use the result to earn broader delegation.

## Current direction

Prove one operated workflow, collapse the interface around the outcome, and use customer evidence to earn adjacent responsibility.

## Domain models

- `.kernel/CODING.md` owns Technical Truth.
- `.kernel/PRODUCT.md` owns the Product Model.
- `.kernel/GTM.md` owns Market Position.

## Non-goals

Do not become a generic agent builder, workflow canvas, dashboard, or marketplace of disconnected automations.
