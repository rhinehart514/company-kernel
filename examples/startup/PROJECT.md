# Project

> Relay completes vendor security reviews for mid-market software companies and produces the proof buyers need to approve them.

**As of:** 2026-08-21

## Outcome

A software company can move from a buyer's security request to an approved, defensible response without coordinating a temporary internal project.

## Why now

Current models can read evidence, map controls, draft bounded responses, detect conflicts, and maintain working context across a review. The remaining scarce pieces are authority, verification, trust, and access to company systems.

## Actors

Security and revenue teams supply evidence and authority. Relay performs the review work. The buyer's security team evaluates the proof. Human owners resolve exceptions and accept risk.

## Core loop

Request arrives → evidence is gathered → answers and artifacts are produced → exceptions are escalated → approval proof is returned → reusable company context improves the next review.

## Responsibility

Relay owns preparation, evidence mapping, consistency, drafting, status, and proof. It does not invent controls, accept legal risk, or make unauthorized commitments.

## Entry and trajectory

The entry is completing individual reviews faster and more reliably. Winning creates a maintained trust context that can become the operating layer for customer assurance, procurement evidence, and external compliance work.

## Boundary

Relay is an operator for customer assurance, not a generic GRC dashboard or a chatbot over policy documents.
