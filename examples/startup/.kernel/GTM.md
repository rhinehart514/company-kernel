# Market Position

**Updated:** 2026-08-21

## Market thesis and targets

Reachable service businesses with expensive repeated administrative work can buy faster than enterprises requiring platform procurement.

## Wedges, buyers, users, and blockers

The entry wedge is one neglected workflow. The owner buys, an operations lead uses it, frontline staff supply source information, and IT can block access.

## Offers, positioning, and messages

The offer is a completed weekly outcome with proof. Position against staffing and fragmented manual work, not against generic software.

## Distribution and sales motion

Founder-led outbound and a live reconstruction of the prospect's workflow lead to a paid pilot with minimal handoff.

## Pricing, packaging, and proof

A monthly operated package includes bounded volume and an explicit exception policy. Proof comes from completed artifacts and measured work removed.

## Pipeline, experiments, and conversion evidence

The current experiment compares problem-led outbound with a prospect-specific live demo. No motion has yet repeated enough to concentrate.

## Objections, competition, and constraints

Buyers fear incorrect external actions and hidden implementation work. Existing staff and agencies are the primary alternatives.

## Bets, direction, and open questions

Concentrate on the workflow with the shortest path to verified completion. Determine whether buyers value labor removed or faster turnaround more strongly.
