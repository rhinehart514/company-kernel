# Product Model

**Updated:** 2026-08-21

## Product thesis

Businesses will delegate bounded workflows when the product owns completion, exposes proof, and preserves judgment at consequential boundaries.

## Actors and outcomes

The operator delegates work, the approver handles exceptions, and the business receives a completed result with evidence.

## Work and responsibility

The product performs the agreed workflow inside granted access. It stops at missing authority, ambiguous policy, or irreversible external commitment.

## Core model, loops, and topology

Delegate work, execute, surface exceptions, complete, prove, and use the result to earn broader delegation. One web surface and one runtime expose the same product model.

## Capabilities, wedges, and bets

The current wedge is one document-heavy operations workflow. The active bet is that proof and recovery matter more than configurability.

## Evidence, assumptions, and constraints

Internal runs show reliable happy-path completion. Customer willingness to delegate without founder support remains unproven.

## Direction, tensions, and non-goals

Move from assisted execution toward bounded operation. Preserve control without turning the product into a workflow builder.
