# Now

## Current outcome

Get three founder-led revenue teams through a live meeting-to-completion loop
with at least one authorized system action and visible proof.

## Active bets

- completion is valuable enough to earn action authority
- an exception-first review creates more trust than reviewing every action
- customer follow-up is the strongest initial responsibility surface

## Recent evidence

Two design partners already copy commitments from meeting notes into the CRM
and manually chase owners. Both described the problem as dropped follow-up,
not missing transcription.

## Open unknowns

- which actions can be pre-authorized on the first use?
- does proof need to be customer-visible or only operator-visible?
- how often do exceptions destroy the time saved?

## Constraint

Do not expand into onboarding or customer success before the sales loop has
completed repeatedly.

## Next proof

Operate five real meetings end to end. Measure work completed, exceptions,
time to completion, human review time, and whether the team delegates the next
meeting without prompting.
