# Relay

Relay operates the follow-up work that starts after a customer meeting and
normally dies across notes, CRM fields, Slack messages, and human memory.

## What must become true

A revenue team leaves every consequential meeting with the right customer
state captured, commitments assigned, systems updated, and next actions
moving without a manager reconstructing the conversation.

The customer buys completed follow-through, not another meeting recorder.

## Why now

Models can understand messy conversations, resolve work against company
systems, generate the required artifacts, and act through tools.

The old product boundary stopped at transcription because reliable action was
too expensive and brittle.

That boundary can move.

## Product model

Relay is an operated follow-through loop:

```text
meeting ends
→ Relay reconstructs commitments and required state
→ work is proposed or executed within authority
→ exceptions reach the right person
→ completion and proof return to the account
```

The interface explains state, exceptions, proof, and control. It does not
expose a workspace full of internal agents.

## Responsibility

Relay owns agreed post-meeting work through completion, exception, or cutoff.

It may update authorized systems, draft and send approved follow-up, assign
work, and monitor completion.

It does not make pricing, legal, personnel, or strategic commitments without
the responsible person's approval.

## Wedge

Start with founder-led B2B teams where every customer conversation matters and
the cost of dropped follow-through is visible.

The wedge earns a larger company: an operating layer for customer work across
sales, onboarding, delivery, and expansion.

## Compounding path

Every completed account creates experience tokens around commitments,
exceptions, system state, and what reliable follow-through requires.

That learning improves defaults, proof, and the amount of work Relay can
responsibly own.

## Decisions already made

- one product model: operated follow-through
- completion is the value metric, not transcripts created
- exceptions are product states, not support failures
- the first experience begins from a real meeting, not a configuration tour
- product and founder-led sales run as one learning loop

## Evidence that should change the model

Reconsider the wedge if teams value summaries but will not grant action
authority after seeing credible proof.

Reconsider the responsibility boundary if exception handling prevents
reliable completion at viable economics.

Kill the direction if completed follow-through does not change retention,
revenue velocity, or meaningful team work.

## Non-goals

- general meeting notes
- a horizontal agent builder
- another CRM
- an automation canvas users must program
