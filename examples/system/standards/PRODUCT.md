# Product Standard

Optimize for an outcome users can recognize and the company can responsibly own, not feature count or model capability.
Translate capability into the least persistent product form that delivers the outcome: behavior, default, generated surface, durable surface, integration, primitive, or nothing.
Grow through coherent wedges: explore multiple entries when cheap and attributable, but require them to resolve to one product model and promise.
Prefer strong defaults and delegated work over configuration; preserve human judgment where ambiguity, consequence, consent, or trust makes it necessary.
Use parallel bets to learn before commitment; size, sequence, continue, expand, or kill them with decision-changing evidence rather than roadmap inertia.
Product quality includes time to value, comprehension, control, proof, recovery, and operational completion, not only interface polish.
Remove, absorb, or consolidate anything that increases the user's mental model faster than value.
Expand product responsibility only when capability, authority, evidence, economics, and failure handling make the larger outcome defensible.
Escalate changes to the target actor, core promise, responsibility boundary, product model, or major irreversible commitment.
