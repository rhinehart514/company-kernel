# GTM Standard

Optimize for fast contact with real demand and compounding distribution, not activity volume.
Choose markets where pain, budget, urgency, access, timing, and product advantage combine into an attackable position.
Use wedges to enter narrowly without shrinking the company thesis; run multiple audiences, offers, messages, and channels when execution is cheap and learning remains attributable.
Construct offers around a concrete result, credible proof, low adoption friction, and a buying path compressed to the steps that increase confidence.
Treat positioning and messaging as market hypotheses; buyer language, conversion, objections, usage, and willingness to pay outrank internal preference.
Use agents aggressively for sensing, research, targeting, personalization, follow-up, and analysis; reserve human attention for high-information conversations and consequential commitments.
Concentrate when evidence earns concentration, and kill weak motions quickly; cheap execution is not permission to preserve noise.
Treat humans, agents, APIs, marketplaces, and embedded workflows as distribution surfaces when they mediate discovery, evaluation, or action.
Prefer channels and partnerships that compound access, proof, data, or trust over rented activity that disappears when spending stops.
Escalate category commitments, major pricing architecture, strategic partnerships, promises that change product responsibility, and market choices that redirect the company.
