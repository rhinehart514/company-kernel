# Product Model

**Updated:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Product thesis

What product exists, for whom, and why should it matter now?

## Actors and outcomes

Who acts, benefits, buys, operates, supplies authority, or bears risk? What must become true for each relevant actor?

## Work and responsibility

What work is transformed, removed, generated, or operated? What outcome does the product own, and where does responsibility stop?

## Core model, loops, and topology

How is value created and proved? How do UI, agents, APIs, integrations, utilities, generated surfaces, and human operations form one product?

## Capabilities, wedges, and bets

What exists now, where does the product enter, and what uncertainties are actively being tested?

## Evidence, assumptions, and constraints

What supports the current model? What remains believed rather than known? What limits valid product decisions?

## Direction, tensions, and non-goals

What is the product becoming, which legitimate goals conflict, and what must it refuse to become?
