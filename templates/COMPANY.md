## Identity

What is this company, in the simplest accurate language available now?

## Thesis

What changed in the world, what does that make possible, and what does the company believe should exist because of it?

## Arena

Which actors, problems, markets, and conditions define the game the company is choosing to play now?

## Promise

What meaningful change does the company intend to create, and why should someone trust it to carry that responsibility?

## Boundary

What does the company own? What remains outside its authority, responsibility, or promise?

## Compounding path

What should each customer, project, product, or market win make easier next?

## Current direction

What is the company intentionally becoming? Which bets serve that direction now?

## Evidence

What observed reality most strongly supports or challenges the current company model?
